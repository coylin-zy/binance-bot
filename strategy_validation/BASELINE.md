# SimpleSpot baseline evidence

This baseline uses the immutable Binance global spot dataset locked by
`dataset-lock.json`. It was generated with Freqtrade `2026.8-dev-a11233e93`, a
0.1% fee per side, a 20 USDT starting balance, and the fixed BTC/USDT, ETH/USDT,
XRP/USDT universe.

| Window | Role | Trades | Return | Max drawdown | Profit factor |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 2024-01-01 to 2024-04-01 | Development | 1 | 0.25% | 0.00% | 0.00 |
| 2024-08-01 to 2024-11-01 | Validation | 2 | 2.19% | 0.00% | 0.00 |
| 2025-01-01 to 2026-07-01 | Sealed holdout | 7 | -2.59% | 3.45% | 0.27 |

- Lookahead analysis checked 7 signals and detected no bias.
- Recursive analysis found 0.000000% variance at the strategy's 200-candle startup.
- The source dataset was generated by GitHub Actions run
  [33133017977](https://github.com/coylin-zy/binance-bot/actions/runs/33133017977).

The bias and recursion gates pass, but the sealed holdout loses money and contains
too few trades to support a durable-edge claim. `SimpleSpot` is therefore not approved
for real-money trading. Keep it in research and long-term dry-run operation.
